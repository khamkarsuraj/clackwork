Free and open source. No account, no telemetry.

What it does

  • Plays a mechanical keyboard sound for every key, in every app
  • Lives in the menu bar; the Dock icon is optional
  • Auto-mutes while the microphone is in use, so calls stay clean
  • Per-app exclusions for the apps that should stay silent
  • Global shortcuts for sound, mute, volume and switching keyboards
  • Launch at login, light and dark mode

Sound profiles

  • 01 Cobalt ClackClicky. Sharp, high-pitched, the loudest
  • 02 Walnut BumpTactile. Soft bump, office-friendly
  • 03 Onyx ThockLinear. Heavy, deep and solid
  • 04 Ember TapLinear. Light, quiet and fast

Install

  1. Open the DMG and drag Thlick to Applications
  2. Launch it, click “Allow Input Monitoring…” and enable Thlick in System Settings
  3. Type. Listening starts on its own within a few seconds

Privacy

The key listener is listen-only. It passes on the key category, such as letter, space or modifier, and nothing else.

Characters are never read, stored or sent anywhere. Password fields are skipped.
